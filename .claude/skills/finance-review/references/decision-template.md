# Decision — template

Use this when the work reaches a fork and someone has to choose. Before you use
it at all, answer one question:

**Is this actually decidable from evidence?**

Most forks presented as judgement calls are not. The two roles on a user, which
of two states is production-derived, whether a grant predates a listener — these
are checkable, and the check decides it. Go and check, then there is no
decision to escalate.

Only escalate a fork that survives that. And when you do, "none of these as
framed" is always available to you and is sometimes the right answer — options
inherit the framing of whoever wrote them, and that framing is often the actual
mistake.

## The fork

<One sentence. What must be decided, and what is blocked until it is.>

## Why it is not decidable from evidence

<What you checked, and why the evidence does not settle it. If you did not
check, go and check.>

## What is actually at stake

<The consequence that differs between the options. If the options differ only in
style, there is no decision — pick one and say you picked it.>

<Name the irreversible one, if any. An option that destroys ground truth, or
that ships to production, or that discards production-derived data, is not on
the same footing as one that does not.>

## Option A — <name>

**Does.** <What happens.>
**Costs.** <What is given up. Be specific about what becomes unknowable.>
**Reversible?** <Yes / no, and how.>

## Option B — <name>

<Same three.>

## Recommendation

<Which, and the one reason that decides it — not a balance of considerations.
If you genuinely cannot recommend, say that, and say what evidence would let you.>

## If the framing is wrong

<If none of the options is right, say so here and give the option that is, with
what the original framing missed. This is not a failure to decide; it is the
decision.>

## What I will do without an answer

<Under unattended operation: the reasonable default you will take, stated up
front, and how to unwind it. If there is no safe default — if every path is
irreversible — do the preparatory work, stop here, and say so.>
